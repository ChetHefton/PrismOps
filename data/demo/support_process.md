# Northstar Industrial Supply support processes

## Order status

- **Manual steps:** Confirm order number and customer identity, look up fulfillment status, copy the carrier update, and send the standard status response.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox, order management system, carrier tracking portal.
- **Common exceptions:** Split shipments, backorders, invalid order numbers, and carrier scans that have not updated.

## Billing question

- **Manual steps:** Verify the account and invoice, compare charges with the order, review payment history, and explain or route the discrepancy.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox, ERP billing module, payment portal.
- **Common exceptions:** Tax-exemption errors, duplicate charges, unapplied credits, and purchase-order mismatches.

## Return request

- **Manual steps:** Validate the purchase and return window, confirm item condition, create an authorization, and provide shipping instructions.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox, order management system, returns portal.
- **Common exceptions:** Custom items, expired windows, partial quantities, and restocking-fee disputes.

## Account access

- **Manual steps:** Verify the user, confirm the account status, trigger a password reset or unlock, and send the standard access instructions.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox, customer portal administration, identity directory.
- **Common exceptions:** Former employees, shared accounts, email-domain changes, and locked parent accounts.

## Damaged shipment

- **Manual steps:** Collect order and damage details, request photographs, review replacement eligibility, open a carrier claim, and arrange replacement or credit.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox, order management system, carrier claims portal, ERP credit module.
- **Common exceptions:** Concealed damage, missing packaging, hazardous materials, high-value orders, and disputed carrier responsibility.

## Product question

- **Manual steps:** Identify the product and intended use, search specifications, confirm compatibility, and send documented guidance or route to a specialist.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox, product information system, internal knowledge base.
- **Common exceptions:** Undocumented compatibility, safety-critical uses, discontinued products, and custom applications.

## Miscellaneous

- **Manual steps:** Read and classify the request, identify an owner, gather missing context, and route or compose a one-off response.
- **Systems used:** Support inbox plus whichever internal system the request requires.
- **Common exceptions:** Requests span departments, lack identifiers, combine multiple issues, or fall outside existing procedures.
